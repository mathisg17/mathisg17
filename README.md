### 🛠️ What I work on

- **[StrucTime](https://structime.app)** : field-oriented SaaS for French SMBs, in production.
  Quotes, invoices, time tracking, payroll and site management across 33 modules.
- **French compliance** : e-invoicing and the 2026 reform, NF525 sealing, GDPR, payroll rules.
- **AI** : model training and fine-tuning, plus StrucTime LWM, a world model for forecasting and
  simulation.
